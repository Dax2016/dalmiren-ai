# Six Continuous Intelligence Loops

DALMIREN AI operates using 6 continuous intelligence loops:

1. **Conversation Loop**: Manages messages, conversation IDs, titles, and thread continuity.
2. **Memory Loop**: Retrieves relevant active memories, extracts candidate facts, and validates database persistence.
3. **Intelligence & Reasoning Loop**: Synthesizes parent query, child profile, and memory context into structured prompts.
4. **Personalised Guidance Loop**: Generates age-appropriate calm responses, hypotheses, and practical tips.
5. **Progress & Follow-Up Loop**: Tracks parenting goals and parent-reported outcomes over time.
6. **Safety & Safeguarding Loop**: Enforces non-diagnostic disclaimers and immediate emergency intervention guidance.
